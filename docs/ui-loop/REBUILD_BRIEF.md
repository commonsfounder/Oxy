## What is open for redesign

- Information architecture: how people find conversation, current work, household context, memory, connected services and controls.
- Navigation: how people move between those capabilities, orient themselves and return. Prefer retaining and improving the wheel spin Chizi likes; reorganise the rest around a coherent flow. Replacement remains an option if supported by the actual design and interaction comparison.
- Layout: composition, density, grouping, alignment, whitespace and what gets the first glance.
- Typography: hierarchy, scale, weight, line length and reading rhythm.
- Controls: what looks tappable, placement, labels, feedback and how consequential choices are presented.
- Interaction: gestures, transitions, progressive disclosure and continuity between states.
- Visual character: colour, shape, depth, assets and selected expressive details that make Adam recognisable.
- Real states: empty, populated, loading, active work, permission requests, outcomes and failures.

Reorganising and replacing existing screens is authorised by this request. Preserve the real capabilities, user data, accessibility and deterministic approval boundaries. A UI rebuild does not imply adding speculative product features, invented sensing or fabricated scores.
