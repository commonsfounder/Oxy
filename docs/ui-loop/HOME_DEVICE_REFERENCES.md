# What apps for home devices do — 27 October 2026

App Store screenshots of 14 apps that go with a device in the home (images kept locally in ~/Documents/Adam-reference-screens/, not in this public repo): Nanit, Owlet, Eight Sleep, Sonos, Tesla, Apple Home, Google Home, Nothing X, Roomba, Philips Hue, ecobee, Ring, Withings, Dyson. Chizi's earlier references (Family, Oura, Gentler Streak, !Boring) are fitness/money/weather apps; their look does not transfer to a home computer.

What these apps share:

1. **The device is drawn first.** Tesla shows the car, Eight Sleep the Pod, Nothing X the headphones, Dyson each product. Readings are labelled around the drawing with thin leader lines (Tesla energy, ecobee).
2. **The home is a map.** Roomba, Dyson and ecobee draw the rooms (isometric or plan) and show state on the map itself: where it cleaned, what is on. Adam's site already promises "a live map without cameras".
3. **One big reading with its control.** Google Home 68 with − / +, ecobee 22, Eight Sleep −2. Big numerals only for a real reading.
4. **A written day summary.** Google Home's "Tuesday's Home Brief" is a paragraph about what happened at home. Adam already writes briefings.
5. **A maintenance-style ask.** Roomba's "It's time to replace the brush" card: picture, one line, one black button, one secondary. That is the shape of an Adam decision.
6. **Live progress with physical controls.** Roomba "Cleaning… 15 min remaining" with round pause / skip / stop buttons.
7. **Events as a timed list, time on the right.** Apple Home Activity, ecobee, Nanit.
8. **An alert takes over the whole screen.** Owlet turns the screen red: "Low oxygen. Check on baby." For Adam: smoke alarm heard, crying, a fall.
9. **Premium devices go dark and product-led** (Eight Sleep, Tesla, Dyson, Nothing); utility homes go light and friendly (Google, Apple, Roomba). Adam's site is dark and product-led.
